## Scene Continuity Rules

For each scene, specify:

- What carries over from the previous scene.
- What changes.
- What must never change.
- What visual detail proves continuity.

## Negative Continuity Rules

Avoid:

- Changing the main subject's identity.
- Changing wardrobe unless explicitly requested.
- Changing product color, label, or proportions.
- Adding new logos.
- Adding extra people unless specified.
- Changing the location architecture.
- Shifting from realistic to stylized footage.
- Overcomplicating motion.
- Creating impossible camera moves.

## Review Checklist

Before exporting a prompt, verify:

- The subject matches the continuity bible.
- The product matches the continuity bible.
- The location remains coherent.
- The camera language is consistent.
- The lighting does not drift.
- The action is simple enough for an 8-10 second clip.
- The end frame creates a usable bridge to the next scene.
