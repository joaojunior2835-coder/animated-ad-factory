# Quality Control Rules

Hard truth: the fastest way to kill an ad — and the brand behind it — is a claim you cannot back. Quality control is not a final polish. It is a set of gates between stages. This document defines them.

These rules apply across all ten stages in `docs/pipeline-stages.md`.

## 1. Claims and Compliance

This is the most important section. It governs every line of copy, voiceover, caption, and on-screen text.

### Default framing

Unless a stronger claim is **explicitly verified and approved** for the specific product, keep all claims framed around:

- **Ritual** — a repeatable, intentional moment.
- **Support** — helping an existing routine, not replacing medicine.
- **Evening routine** — the time and habit, not a physiological outcome.
- **Control** — feeling in charge of a moment.
- **Alignment** — staying consistent with the user's own training or lifestyle goals.

### Prohibited by default

Do not make, imply, or visually suggest:

- **Medical claims** — treating, curing, preventing, or diagnosing any condition.
- **Weight-loss claims** — losing weight, burning fat, slimming, body transformation.
- **Guaranteed appetite suppression** — "kills cravings", "suppresses hunger", "coupe-faim", or any promise that the product removes appetite.
- **Before/after** — body comparisons, scales, weight numbers, measuring tapes, calorie counts.
- **Guaranteed or universal results** — "works for everyone", "guaranteed", specific timelines for an outcome.
- **Superiority or disparagement** — unverified comparisons to competitors or to medication.

### Escalation

If the user wants a stronger claim, do not write it on assumption. Flag it, state what verification it would require (clinical substantiation, regulatory approval, documented evidence), and keep the safe framing until that verification is explicitly provided.

### Visual compliance

The claim rules apply to images and motion, not just words. No scales, no shrinking bodies, no "fat to fit" transitions, no clinical/pharmaceutical staging that implies a medical product.

## 2. Localization

- Language and market are set at stage 1 and are non-negotiable downstream.
- Voiceover, captions, and any on-screen text must be in the ad's language.
- Copy must read as written by a native speaker for that market, not as a literal translation.
- Idioms, claim norms, and tone must fit the target market's expectations and regulations.

## 3. Continuity

Carried from the continuity bible and enforced on every visual stage:

- Subject identity, wardrobe, and expression range stay consistent.
- Product shape, color, materials, and label stay consistent and visible as required.
- Location, lighting, color palette, and camera language do not drift.
- Each end frame must create a usable bridge into the next beat (see the export's Bridge Check).

## 4. Per-Stage Quality Bars

- **Intake:** benefit stated with no prohibited claim; market and language set.
- **Audience psychology:** exactly one persona and one core tension.
- **Ad concept:** one idea, one angle, one visual style.
- **Story beats:** opens on a scroll-stopper, ends on a product hero, one idea per beat.
- **Frame prompts:** still images only; continuity preserved; end frame bridges forward.
- **Flow clip prompts:** motion between two frames only; no invented scenes; one primary action per clip.
- **Voiceover:** correct language; on-beat; compliant; speakable in the clip's duration.
- **Music direction:** energy curve matches the emotional arc.
- **Edit plan:** clip order matches beats; total runtime equals the sum of clip durations.
- **Export:** validation passes; no leftover template variables; copy-paste ready.

## 5. Review Gates

Before exporting, verify:

- No prohibited claims in any voiceover line, caption, concept, or frame prompt.
- All language is correct for the market.
- The product is visually identical across every clip.
- The subject is visually identical across every clip.
- Lighting and style stay coherent.
- Each clip has one primary action.
- Each end frame bridges into the next beat.
- Duration math is clean and the package is copy-paste ready.

A project that fails any claim or localization gate does not ship, regardless of how strong the visuals are.
