# Brand Test Doc

- **Brand voice:** warm, direct, feminine, disciplined.
- **Audience:** women in France who train but struggle with evening cravings.
- **Claims restriction:** do not claim weight loss, appetite suppression, medical results, or guaranteed craving control.
- **Visual identity:** warm tactile claymation, beige, cream, cocoa brown, soft pink.
- **Offer:** evening ritual drink positioned around routine, control, and consistency.
